# Notices

This repository holds the browser host and the deployment of the WinUI Gallery of FerroUI. The
gallery itself is the sample `samples/WinUIGallery` of the FerroUI submodule, and its notices apply
to the site:

- [FerroUI/samples/WinUIGallery/NOTICE.md](https://github.com/wieslawsoltes/FerroUI/blob/main/samples/WinUIGallery/NOTICE.md):
  the catalog, descriptions, sample sources and media of Microsoft WinUI Gallery that the sample
  derives from, with their licence.
- [FerroUI/NOTICE.md](https://github.com/wieslawsoltes/FerroUI/blob/main/NOTICE.md): the notices of
  FerroUI and of the components it builds on.
